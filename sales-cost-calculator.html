How it's calculated

  1. Pipeline SDRs aren't booking: (target - actual meetings) x 12 x SDRs x close rate x deal value. Revenue from the meetings they miss, at your own close rate.
  2. Revenue closers are missing: AEs x quota x (1 - attainment). The gap between quota and what they actually close.
  3. Ramp and churn on SDRs: SDRs x attrition x (monthly pay x ramp months x 50%). A replacement produces about half output while ramping.
  4. Management firefighting: managers x pay x (hours ÷ 40). Leadership time on basics instead of strategy.
  5. Marketing that goes nowhere: spend x wasted share. Budget that never becomes real pipeline.

Sources and honesty

  • SDR pay, ramp, attrition, meeting target and manager pay: The Bridge Group, SDR Models, Motions & Metrics 2025 (351 B2B companies).
  • Everything tagged example is a placeholder for you to replace. The total mixes missed revenue with wasted spend on purpose: both are money the broken machine costs you.
  • This is an estimate to start a conversation, not an audit. The real number comes from looking at your actual pipeline.
