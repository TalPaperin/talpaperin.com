Fractional CRO

A fractional CRO owns the revenue number: strategy, the sales team, the pipeline, marketing that feeds it, and the forecast. I sit in that seat for B2B companies, including B2B SaaS, without a $250,000-plus full-time hire. Week one I diagnose what is broken. Week two you get the plan. Then I run it.

Revenue stuck? Tell me where. A message, WhatsApp or a short call, whichever suits you.

When you need a fractional CRO

You need CRO-level judgment, but the work is not yet a full week. Or you need results now and cannot wait two quarters for a full-time hire to ramp. That is the gap a fractional CRO fills.

  • Revenue has stalled and nobody actually owns the number
  • You are scaling and need a leader before you can justify a full-time CRO
  • A full-time CRO runs $250,000-plus all in, with months to find and ramp and real severance risk

What I own

  • Revenue strategy, the forecast and the accountability, end to end
  • The go-to-market motion, the pipeline and the team that runs it
  • Marketing that actually feeds pipeline, not just activity
  • The hard calls: who to hire, who to train, who to replace

How it works

Week one I diagnose where deals stall and leak. Week two you have a plan and a real forecast. Week three I am running it. You get a senior operator who has done this 30-plus times on five continents, not a strategy deck that sits in a drawer.

Priced by how much of me you need

From a weekly advisor to a fully embedded CRO. The more of me you take, the more I own. The only thing that changes is how much of my week is on your business.

  • Advisor, $2,000 a month: about three hours a week, a standing strategy call plus access between calls
  • Starter, $6,000 a month: two hours a day, every working day
  • Growth, $12,000 a month: half time, four hours a day, hands-on. Full-time and exclusive available on request
  • On top of every level, a performance share measured against a baseline we agree up front. The full breakdown is on the pricing page

Common questions

What is a fractional CRO?

A senior revenue leader who owns your strategy, team, pipeline, forecast and the number, part time, without a full-time CRO salary or a long-term lock-in.

What does a fractional CRO do?

Owns the revenue number and the work behind it: the sales strategy and forecast, the go-to-market motion, the pipeline, and the team that runs it. Day to day that means diagnosing where deals stall, building the playbook, hiring, training and managing reps, inspecting the pipeline, and stepping into the big deals. Not advice from the side. Someone in the seat who answers for the result.

When should you hire a fractional CRO?

When revenue has stalled and nobody senior owns the number, when you need results before you can justify a $250,000 full-time hire, or after you have burned money on VP hires that did not move the number. If you only need more hands, hire reps. If you need someone to own the strategy, the team and the forecast, that is a fractional CRO.

How is a fractional CRO different from a consultant?

A consultant hands you a deck and leaves. I do the work, in the pipeline and the deals, and answer for the result.

How much does a fractional CRO cost?

$2,000 to $12,000 a month depending on how many hours a day you need, versus $250,000-plus for a full-time CRO, billed monthly with no contract.
