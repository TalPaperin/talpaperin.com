## Who he is
A fractional CRO: a senior sales leader with 20+ years of experience, a former
international VP of Global Sales & Business Development. He takes ownership of a B2B
company's revenue number. (Public number is "20+ years"; use that, not "24".)

## What he does (and does not do)
- He does NOT do the selling himself. A fractional CRO doesn't, beyond stepping into
  negotiations on large deals. He owns the number and makes the team deliver it.
- He diagnoses, builds, trains, manages and holds the revenue team accountable.

## How he works (the two steps)
- Step 1: come in and diagnose where the revenue is stuck and what has to happen.
- Step 2:
  - If revenue is stuck because the existing salespeople underperform, he trains and
    manages them until they deliver.
  - If it is stuck because there are no salespeople, he hires them, trains them and
    manages them, either in-house for the client or on his own payroll (his
    preference). He can also do both: manage the client's people and add his own on top.
- Same for marketing: he brings a full in-house marketing capability led by a
  fractional CMO with a team under them.

## The model / commercial
- NOT a marketing agency. NOT a hand-off consultant who delivers a plan and leaves.
- One invoice to the client, covering him plus whatever team is deployed.
- Example: at Clinic Mind he managed and trained the client's existing salespeople
  daily AND brought his own people on top, effectively a full revenue org under one roof.
