# Explee standing disqualifiers (leads NOT worth pursuing)

Rules learned from real outcomes. The reply-check and EOD-handoff routines read
this file each run and apply it. A prospect who matches a disqualifier is NOT a
fit: do NOT auto-reply to book, do NOT push the Calendly link, do NOT hand them
to an SDR. Surface the lead to Tal and let him decide. Add new disqualifiers here
as they come up (one short rule per bullet) and commit.

- COMMISSION-ONLY / SUCCESS-FEE-ONLY. Tal works on a fractional retainer, not on
  pure commission, a cut of the deals, pay-on-results, or equity-only. A prospect
  who only wants to engage on commission / a cut of deals / success fee with no
  retainer is a waste of time (this is what the Oren Haimov call turned out to be).
  Skip and surface to Tal; do not book a call around it.
