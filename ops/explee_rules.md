# Explee standing rules: disqualifiers + how to qualify BEFORE booking

Rules learned from real outcomes. The reply-check and EOD-handoff routines read
this file each run and apply it. Add new lessons here (one short rule per bullet)
and commit. Keep everything short: prospects do NOT read long emails or long
meeting invites, so qualification has to be done in one plain line, not a wall of text.

## Disqualifiers (NOT a fit: do not auto-book, do not push Calendly, do not hand to an SDR; surface to Tal)

- COMMISSION-ONLY / SUCCESS-FEE-ONLY / REV-SHARE / EQUITY-ONLY. Tal works on a
  fractional retainer. He does NOT work for pure commission, a cut of the deals,
  pay-on-results or equity only. This has already burned two booked calls that
  turned out to be commission-only time-wasters (Oren Haimov, and Mohan Sam /
  Spenego). If a prospect's reply hints they want a commission / rev-share /
  success-fee / equity setup, do NOT book a call: surface to Tal.

## Qualify in the reply, before the call (this is the lesson from the wasted calls)

The wasted calls happened because people booked without understanding Tal charges
a retainer, and the long confirmation email / calendar invite did not fix it
because they do not read it. So set the frame in the REPLY itself:

- TALPAPERIN booking replies: whenever a reply to a warm Talpaperin lead includes
  the Calendly link, add ONE short, matter-of-fact line stating the engagement
  model so commission-seekers self-select out before they take a slot. Keep it to
  a single plain sentence, senior and non-defensive, e.g.: "Quick note so we don't
  waste the call: I work on a fractional retainer, not commission or rev-share."
  Do NOT turn this into a paragraph and do NOT add it to HireMatch replies.
- If, after that line, a prospect still pushes for commission-only terms, stop:
  surface to Tal, do not keep booking.
