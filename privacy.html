Privacy

What this site collects, why, and how to get it deleted. No legalese.

Who is responsible

This site is run by Tal Paperin, who also works through KSW Solutions. For anything about your data, write to tal@ksw.solutions.

What I collect, and when

  • When you send a message through the contact form: your name, your email address and your message.
  • When you book a call: what you enter in the booking window, such as your name, email, phone number and your answers to the booking questions.
  • When you subscribe to the newsletter: your email address.
  • When you submit the form on the sales cost calculator: your name, your email address, your company if you give it, the figures you entered, and the PDF of them that you receive. If you do not submit the form, nothing you type into the calculator leaves your browser.
  • When you email or WhatsApp me: whatever you choose to write.
  • When you browse: the pages you view, the page you came from, your approximate location, and your device and browser type. This comes from analytics.

The quote builder on the pricing page runs in your browser. Its email button opens your own email program, so nothing is sent until you send that email yourself.

Why I collect it

To reply to you, to hold the call you booked, to send the newsletter you asked for, and to see which pages are useful so I can improve the site. I do not sell your data, rent it or trade it.

Who processes it for me

I use outside services to run the site. Each handles data under its own privacy policy, and some are based in the United States, so your data may be processed outside your country.

  • Contact form and calculator submissions are emailed to me through my own Google mailbox. Formspree is the backup route if that fails.
  • Calendly and Zoom schedule and host the calls.
  • beehiiv holds the newsletter list.
  • Google Analytics and Ahrefs Web Analytics produce traffic statistics.
  • YouTube serves the embedded videos, and sees your IP address when one loads.
  • Vercel hosts the site and keeps standard server logs.
  • Google and WhatsApp carry the email and messages you send me.

Cookies

If you visit from the EU, the UK or elsewhere in Europe, the site asks first: Google Analytics sets no cookies unless you accept them in the banner, and you can change your choice any time in the cookie settings. Elsewhere, Google Analytics sets cookies to tell one visit from another. Anywhere, you can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, or install Google's opt-out browser add-on, and the site works the same. Calendly and YouTube may set their own cookies when you open the booking window or play a video.

How long I keep it

  • Messages and booking details: while we are in contact, and afterwards as needed for my business records.
  • Newsletter: until you unsubscribe, which you can do from any issue.
  • Analytics: for the retention period set by each analytics provider.

Your choices

You can ask me for a copy of the personal data I hold about you, ask me to correct it, or ask me to delete it. Email tal@ksw.solutions and I will handle it. If you are in the EU or the UK, you can also complain to your local data protection authority.
