Founders ask me this constantly. Should we hire SDRs or outsource outbound? The honest answer is that it depends on your stage, and most early-stage companies pick wrong because they are copying what a Series C company did, not what fits where they actually are.

Let me tell you what you are really buying with each.

## What in-house actually costs you

In-house SDRs give you control and culture. Your reps live your product, sit in your Slack, and improve your pitch in real time. That is genuinely valuable, and eventually most companies want it.

But be honest about the bill. An in-house SDR is not one salary. It is recruiting time, a ramp of three to six months before they produce, a manager who knows how to coach outbound, tooling, data, and the very real chance that your first hire does not work out and you start over. At the earliest stage you are paying full price for months before you see a single booked meeting, and you are the one debugging their sequences at 9pm.

If you have product-market fit, a repeatable motion, and a leader who has built an SDR team before, in-house is the right investment. If you are still figuring out who buys and why, you are building a factory before you know what you are manufacturing.

## What outsourced actually buys you

Outsourced outbound, and especially pay-per-meeting, buys you speed and results-based cost. You skip the recruiting, the ramp, and the management overhead. You get conversations with real buyers this month, and in the pay-per-meeting model you pay for the outcome, not the effort.

That last part matters for a cash-constrained company. A retainer pays the same whether meetings land or not. A results-based model puts the risk where it belongs. My [pay-per-meeting service](/services/pay-per-meeting) exists for exactly this reason: you pay for qualified meetings booked, not for a dashboard full of activity.

The tradeoff is real. You get less day-to-day control and the vendor does not absorb your product the way an employee would. That is why the best use of outsourced outbound early on is to learn the market fast, prove the motion works, and only then decide whether to bring it in-house.

## Choose by stage, not by ego

Here is the simple rule I give founders:

- **Pre product-market fit or first outbound motion.** Outsource or go pay-per-meeting. Buy speed and learning, keep cash risk low.
- **Repeatable motion, known ICP, ready to scale.** Start building in-house so the knowledge compounds inside your walls.
- **Somewhere in between.** Run a hybrid. Use [outsourced sales](/services/outsourced-sales) to keep pipeline flowing while you hire and ramp your first internal reps.

Do not hire an SDR team to feel like a real company. Buy the outcome you need for the stage you are in. The org chart can wait.
